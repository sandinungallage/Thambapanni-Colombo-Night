Why Thambapanni

The island's oldest name

Thambapanni is the name Sri Lanka carried in ancient times. We borrowed it for a night that puts the island's music, dance and food in one place, in the middle of Colombo.

The first edition filled the courtyard of Arcade Independence Square. Phase 2 is planned for 2027.

    Culture, up close

    Heritage Moves opens the evening. Performers in masks and traditional costume work the floor around the Heritage Moves stage, close enough to feel the heat when the fire comes out.

    Entrance is free. You do not need a concert ticket to watch, so bring family and friends and arrive early.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Is Phase 2 ticketed?

    The main concert was ticketed in Phase 1, with Heritage Moves open to everyone with free entrance. We expect the same structure in 2027 and will confirm it with the ticket release.

    Where will Phase 2 be held?

    The venue for 2027 is still to be announced. Phase 1 took place at Arcade Independence Square, Colombo.

    Can I bring my own drinks?

    Phase 1 tickets were BYOB. The Phase 2 policy will be published with the ticket details.

    What time should I arrive?

    In 2025, Heritage Moves started at 5:00 PM, doors opened at 6:30 PM and the main show began at 7:00 PM.

    Can I perform or set up a stall?

    Contact the team through the Contact page with your details. Heritage Moves performers, food stalls and partners are all welcome to ask.
