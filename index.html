Why Thambapanni

The island's oldest name

Thambapanni is the name Sri Lanka carried in ancient times. We borrowed it for a night that puts the island's music, dance and food in one place, in the middle of Colombo.

The first edition filled the courtyard of Arcade Independence Square. Phase 2 is planned for 2027.
